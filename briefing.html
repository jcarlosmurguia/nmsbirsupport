SBA & federal agencies

Agency proposal caps take effect with FY2027 on October 1 — plan submissions selectively

Under the Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971, signed April 13, 2026), agencies must set their own limits on how many Phase I and certain Phase II proposals a single company can submit, on a per-year, per-solicitation or per-topic basis, beginning in FY2027. HHS has already moved to nine submissions per business per fiscal year. The law also created Strategic Breakthrough Awards up to $30 million, expanded foreign-risk screening, and allows agencies to carry unobligated FY2026 funds into FY2027.

Congress · S. 3971

Reauthorization background: SBIR/STTR run through 2031 after a six-month lapse

The programs lapsed October 1, 2025 and were restored when the Senate passed S. 3971 unanimously on March 3, the House approved it 345–41 on March 17, and the President signed it April 13, 2026. Agencies are now compressing solicitations before the fiscal year ends September 30, which is why deadlines are stacking up in September and October.
