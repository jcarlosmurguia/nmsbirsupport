NIH omnibus receipt date is Tuesday — first standard due date since the freeze

NIH’s FY2026 parent SBIR and STTR NOFOs carry a September 5 standard receipt date that rolls to Tuesday, September 8, 2026 (5:00pm applicant-organization local time) because the 5th is a Saturday and the 7th is Labor Day. DOE Genesis Mission Phase I: 700-word pitch due Thursday, Sep 10

DOE’s first pitch-first SBIR/STTR opportunity closes September 10 at 2:00pm ET. Four topics — AI for biotechnology and biomanufacturing, AI for quantum computing and networking, AI-driven materials inverse design, and AI-driven autonomous laboratories — will fund about 40 Phase I awards of up to $250K each. Cross-cutting policy

FY2027 starts in 25 days: proposal caps and foreign-risk screening take formal effect

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (signed April 13, 2026) authorizes SBIR/STTR through September 30, 2031. Agency per-company proposal caps take formal effect in FY2027 (HHS already caps at nine per fiscal year), every application now undergoes mandatory foreign-risk due diligence on ownership, patents, personnel and financial ties, and Strategic Breakthrough Awards of up to $30M with 100% match are appearing in NIH and NSF solicitations. Agencies are compressing remaining FY2026 solicitations ahead of the September 30 fiscal-year end.
