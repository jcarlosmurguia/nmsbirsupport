Congress / SBA

Fiscal year-end watch: unspent FY2026 SBIR/STTR funds carry into FY2027; agency proposal caps start next year

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (signed April 13, 2026) extends SBIR/STTR through September 30, 2031 and lets agencies with SBIR/STTR funds left at the end of FY2026 use them in FY2027 — important after a lapse that compressed the year’s obligations into five months. Beginning in FY2027 (October 1), agencies will set their own per-company, per-solicitation or per-topic proposal caps, and every application faces expanded foreign-risk screening. Expect an unusually heavy end-of-September award push and new caps in the first FY2027 solicitations.
