Department of Energy

DOE Genesis Mission Phase I: 700-word pitch due Thursday, Sep 10 at 2:00pm ET

DOE’s first pitch-first SBIR/STTR opportunity under the Office of Technology Commercialization and its intermediary ConnectWerx closes September 10. Four AI-centered topics — scaling biotechnology and biomanufacturing, AI for quantum computing and networking, materials inverse design, and autonomous laboratories — will fund roughly 40 Phase I awards of up to $250K from a $10M pool. DOE $147M FY2025 Phase II opportunity for prior DOE awardees closes Sep 25 at noon ET

Alongside the Genesis Mission pitch, DOE opened a separate Phase II opportunity worth up to $147,394,250 for eligible prior DOE Phase I, Phase II or follow-on awardees, due September 25, 2026 at 12:00pm ET. Cross-agency policy

FY2027 begins October 1: expect a September rush and new proposal caps

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971), passed by the Senate March 3 and the House March 17 (345–41) and signed April 13, reauthorizes SBIR/STTR through September 30, 2031 after a six-month lapse. It adds Strategic Breakthrough Awards (a $30M commercialization pathway), proposal caps and expanded security due-diligence requirements. Because agencies are trying to obligate FY2026 funds before September 30, several are compressing solicitations into this month — which is why NIH, DOE, ARPA-H and DoW deadlines are all stacked in the next three weeks.
