NSF FY2026 SBIR/STTR Phase I open — proposals due July 27

Two solicitations are live: NSF 26-510 (deep technologies advancing U.S. competitiveness & security) and NSF 26-511, a new $40M pilot for next-generation scientific instrumentation. Congress · SBA

SBIR/STTR reauthorized through September 30, 2031

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971) was signed into law April 13, 2026, extending both programs through FY2031 — the longest reauthorization in the programs' recent history — and ending the six-month lapse that began September 30, 2025. It authorizes roughly $6 billion in program funding and lets agencies carry unobligated FY2026 funds into FY2027.

Two changes matter for applicants: a new “Strategic Breakthrough” allocation letting large-budget agencies fund milestone-based awards up to $30M per company, and expanded national-security & supply-chain due diligence — including screening for foreign affiliations in countries of concern — before award.

NIH · Seed

NIH reissues omnibus after a rare hiatus — first deadline Sep 8

For the first time in a decade NIH entered the summer with no active SBIR/STTR omnibus. The FY26 reset reposted the parent series (PA-27-100) on June 1 with a $3M Phase I/II cap and a first standard receipt date of September 8, 2026 (shifted from Sep 5 for the weekend and Labor Day). The reset also launches a Direct-to-Phase II STTR pathway — the most consequential change in years for university-affiliated small businesses, a strong fit for New Mexico's lab-adjacent life-science startups.

NASA on a rolling BAA — PY2026 Phase I closed May 21; next appendix ahead
