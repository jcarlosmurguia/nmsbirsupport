NIH omnibus receipt date is nine days out — the single most urgent bioscience deadline on this page

NIH reopened its small-business submission window on August 5, 2026 with a reissued parent series: PA-27-100 (SBIR), PA-27-102 (STTR), PA-27-101 (Phase IIB Strategic Breakthrough) and PAR-27-098 (Commercialization Readiness Pilot). National Cancer Institute

NCI’s Innovative Concept Award runs as a contract solicitation — a different bid path than the omnibus

The NCI SBIR Innovative Concept Phase I award program closes September 21, 2026 and is administered as a contract solicitation through SAM.gov rather than as a grant through eRA Commons. That distinction matters operationally: the proposal format, evaluation criteria and registration path all differ from the NIH omnibus, and a company set up only for grant submission is not automatically ready to bid. Worth a look for New Mexico oncology, diagnostics and bio-instrumentation companies that want a second bioscience shot this fall.

DoD Release 26.5 is open now and closes September 23 — 26.6 pre-released September 2

DoD’s monthly-cadence BAA structure means two windows are live at once. Release 5 pre-released August 5, opened for submission August 26, and closes September 23, 2026 with roughly 54 topics. Release 6 pre-releases September 2, opens September 23 and closes October 21, 2026. DOE opened a $10 million FY2026 SBIR/STTR Phase I opportunity on July 22, 2026, covering four technology areas tied to the Genesis Mission and expected to yield roughly 40 awards of up to $200,000 each over about six months. NSF’s next full-proposal deadline is November 4 — but the Project Pitch gate comes first

NSF’s current cycle runs full-proposal deadlines on roughly a four-month cadence: July 27 and November 4, 2026, then March 4, 2027. Only companies whose Project Pitch is judged a good fit are invited to submit a full proposal, and that review turnaround has to be planned as its own lead time — a company that starts the pitch in late October will not make the November window. Congress · SBA

Reauthorization through 2031 is settled — the live question is now the FY2027 per-company proposal caps

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act of 2026 was signed April 13, 2026, extending SBIR and STTR through September 30, 2031 after a six-month lapse that froze new awards. Beyond restoring the programs, the Act creates a Phase II “strategic breakthrough” vehicle with a $30 million ceiling, tightens foreign risk screening, and lets agencies carry unspent FY2026 SBIR/STTR funds into FY2027. The consequential change for NM companies lands next fiscal year: beginning in FY2027, each agency sets its own caps on how many proposals a single company may submit per solicitation or per topic. Firms that have relied on high-volume submission strategies should be rethinking them now.
