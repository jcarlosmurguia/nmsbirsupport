SBIR/STTR reauthorized through September 30, 2031

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971) is law, reauthorizing both programs through FY2031 and ending the lapse that began when authorization expired September 30, 2025. It authorizes roughly $6 billion in funding and cleared the House 345–41.

Two reforms matter for applicants: a new Phase II "Strategic Breakthrough" award category for agencies spending over $100M/yr on SBIR, and expanded national-security and supply-chain due diligence on awardees before award.

NSF FY2026 SBIR/STTR Phase I open — proposals due July 27

Two solicitations are live: NSF 26-510 (deep technologies advancing U.S. competitiveness & security) and NSF 26-511 (a new pilot on scientific instrumentation). Phase I awards up to $305,000 (6–18 months); Phase II up to $1,250,000 (~24 months).

Reminder: the mandatory Project Pitch on-ramp reopened June 2 — a Phase I proposal cannot be submitted without an invited pitch first.

Army FUZExTech|Phantum

Army launches five new SBIR/STTR opportunities

Following reauthorization, the Army opened five funding opportunities under its Army FUZE initiative. One is the xTech|Phantum competition: up to 16 finalists receive $2,500 each to pitch in Arizona this July; up to 8 winners get an additional $20,000 and may submit a Phase I proposal worth up to $300,000 (≤6-month proof-of-concept). New topics pre-release July 1, open July 22, close August 19.
