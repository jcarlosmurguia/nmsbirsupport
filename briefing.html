Congress · SBA

SBIR/STTR reauthorized through September 30, 2031

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971) is law, reauthorizing both programs through FY2031 and ending the lapse that began September 30, 2025. It authorizes roughly $6 billion and cleared the House 345–41.

Two reforms matter for applicants: a new Phase II "Strategic Breakthrough" award for agencies spending over $100M/yr on SBIR, and expanded national-security and supply-chain due diligence before award.

National Science Foundation

NSF FY2026 SBIR/STTR Phase I open — proposals due July 27

Two solicitations are live: NSF 26-510 (deep technologies advancing U.S. competitiveness & security) and NSF 26-511 (a new pilot on scientific instrumentation). Phase I up to $305,000 (6–18 months); Phase II up to $1,250,000 (~24 months). Deep-tech scope spans all four NM FAST sectors.

Reminder: the mandatory Project Pitch on-ramp reopened June 2 — a Phase I proposal cannot be submitted without an invited pitch first.

U.S. Army

Army launches five new SBIR/STTR opportunities (xTech|Phantum)

Following reauthorization, the Army opened five funding opportunities under its Army FUZE initiative. The xTech|Phantum competition awards up to 16 finalists $2,500 each to pitch in Arizona this July; up to 8 winners get an additional $20,000 and may submit a Phase I proposal up to $300,000 (≤6-month proof-of-concept). New topics pre-release July 1, open July 22, close August 19.

Dept. of Energy · OTC

DOE SBIR/STTR: Phase II and new Phase I windows this summer

DOE's SBIR/STTR programs are now run by the Office of Technology Commercialization. DOE plans a Phase II opportunity for eligible Phase I awardees in summer 2026, followed later in the summer by a new Phase I opportunity across DOE priority areas. Topics span basic energy sciences, advanced scientific computing, fusion, and nuclear.
