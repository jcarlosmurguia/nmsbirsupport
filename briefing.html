NASA rolling BAA: 2026A/B selections pending, watch for the next appendix

NASA’s SBIR/STTR BAA (released April 17, 2026, valid through September 30, 2027) replaced the single annual solicitation with phased appendices. Appendices 2026A SBIR, 2026B SBIR and 2026B STTR closed May 21; NASA has said additional appendices will be announced later, and proposal limits reset with each one. Cross-agency policy

FY2027 begins October 1: proposal caps and foreign-risk screening take formal effect

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act of 2026 (S. 3971), signed April 13 after a six-month lapse, reauthorizes SBIR/STTR through September 30, 2031. Beginning in FY2027 each agency must set limits on how many Phase I and Phase II proposals a company can submit — per fiscal year, per solicitation or per topic — within 90 days of the fiscal year start, and all applicants face expanded foreign-risk due diligence. The law also created the Phase II Strategic Breakthrough award (up to $30M with a 100% match) and lets agencies carry unspent FY2026 SBIR/STTR funds into FY2027, which is why agencies are compressing solicitations into September.
