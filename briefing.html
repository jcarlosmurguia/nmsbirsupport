Congress · SBA

SBIR/STTR reauthorized through September 30, 2031

The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971) was signed into law April 13, 2026, extending both programs through FY2031 and ending the lapse that began September 30, 2025. It authorizes roughly $6 billion in program funding.

Two changes matter for applicants: a new Phase II "Strategic Breakthrough" award with a $30M ceiling, and expanded national-security & supply-chain due diligence — including screening for foreign affiliations in countries of concern — before award. Beginning in FY2027, agencies will also set their own per-company proposal caps.

National Science Foundation

NSF FY2026 SBIR/STTR Phase I open — proposals due July 27

Two solicitations are live: NSF 26-510 (deep technologies advancing U.S. competitiveness & security) and NSF 26-511, a new ~$40M pilot for next-generation scientific instrumentation. NSF reopened the program with roughly $250M and a July 27, 2026 full-proposal deadline; subsequent deadlines fall November 4, 2026 and March 4, 2027. Phase I awards run up to $305,000.

The deep-tech scope spans all four NM FAST sectors. Remember the mandatory Project Pitch on-ramp — a Phase I proposal cannot be submitted without an invited pitch first.

DoD Release 3 & DARPA topics close July 22

Following April's reauthorization, the DoD/DoW pushed out 115 open and pre-release topics on DSIP and moved to a monthly BAA cadence (topics pre-release the first Wednesday of each month). U.S. Army

Army launches five new SBIR/STTR opportunities (xTech|Phantum)

The Army opened five funding opportunities under its FUZE initiative after reauthorization. The xTech|Phantum competition awards up to 16 finalists $2,500 each to pitch this July; up to 8 winners get an additional $20,000 and may submit a Phase I proposal up to $300,000 for a ≤6-month proof-of-concept. New topics pre-released July 1, open July 22, and close August 19.

Dept. of Energy · OTC

DOE SBIR/STTR: new Phase I window expected later this summer

DOE's SBIR/STTR programs are now run by the Office of Technology Commercialization. DOE plans to open Phase I opportunities later in summer 2026 across current priority areas — basic energy sciences, advanced scientific computing, fusion, and nuclear. Phase I awards provide up to roughly $200,000 for about six months of feasibility work; a required Letter of Intent precedes the full application, so watch the portal for LOI dates.

NASA

NASA on a rolling BAA — PY2026 Phase I closed May 21; next appendix ahead

NASA's SBIR/STTR program moved to a Broad Agency Announcement model with phased appendix releases rather than one annual solicitation. The BAA released April 17, 2026 is valid through September 30, 2027; the PY2026 Phase I proposal window closed May 21, 2026, so the current action is watching for the next appendix release. NIH · Seed

NIH reissues omnibus PA-27-100 after a rare hiatus — first deadline Sep 5

For the first time in a decade NIH entered the summer with no active SBIR/STTR omnibus. The FY26 reset reposted the parent SBIR omnibus (PA-27-100) reopening the ~$1.4B small-business pipeline, with Phase I/II caps raised to $3M and a first standard deadline of September 5, 2026 (observed September 8 given the holiday). The reset also introduces Direct-to-Phase II STTR awards.
