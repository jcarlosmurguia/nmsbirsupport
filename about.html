What is NM FAST?

A statewide team, helping
NM founders win federal SBIR.

NM FAST (Federal and State Technology Partnership Program) is funded by the New Mexico Economic Development Department and operated out of Arrowhead Center at NMSU. Our job is to get more NM startups across the SBIR/STTR finish line — especially first-time applicants, rural teams, and underserved founders.

Mission

Make the federal R&D funding ladder
accessible to every NM founder.

The federal SBIR/STTR program puts roughly $4 billion a year into small-business R&D. New Mexico's share of that is growing — but the proposal process is a wall for most first-time applicants. NM FAST lowers the wall.

We meet founders wherever they are: pre-idea, pre-SBIR, mid-draft, or post-Phase-II. Every program below is free to NM companies.

Partners

Who we work with.

NMEDD

The New Mexico Economic Development Department funds NM FAST through the federal FAST grant and administers the state SBIR matching grant.

Larta Institute

National SBIR commercialization training partner. Provides structured workshops and 1-on-1 coaching for NM teams.

Los Alamos Makers

Prototyping and community-based technical expertise in northern NM for hardware-heavy teams.

APEX Accelerators

Federal contracting and SBIR outreach partner. Co-hosts the Cybersecurity for SBIR workshop.

Santa Fe Business Incubator (SFBI)

Leads the Commercialization Bridge II–III cohort, supporting NM companies transitioning from Phase II to Phase III.

Participating Federal Agencies

DOE, NSF, NASA, DoD, NIH, USDA, NOAA, DHS, DOT, and others. We coordinate directly with program managers when it helps an NM applicant.

Funding

Where the money comes from.

NM FAST is funded through the federal FAST (Federal and State Technology Partnership) grant, administered by the SBA and matched by NMEDD. That's why every program on this site is free to NM-based applicants.
